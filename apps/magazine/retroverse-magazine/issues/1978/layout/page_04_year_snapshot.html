Year Snapshot: 1978 in Motion: Dance Floors, Marquees, and Living Rooms

The year at a glance

By A.J. Hunter

1978 feels like a hinge year, where radio hits such as Night Fever carry club energy into everyday streets, cabs, and corner stores.

Artists including Bee Gees and Donna Summer are proving that polished production can still feel immediate, social, and physical.

At the multiplex, Grease and Superman confirm that theatrical scale matters, even as Mork and Mindy keeps families tied to weekly television habits.
