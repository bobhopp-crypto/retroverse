Movies of the Year

By Martin Hale, Film Editor

If you want to feel moviegoing energy in this 1978 context build, start with the crossover songs. "You're The One That I Want" (John Travolta) appears at rank 15 in the top songs set with 24 chart weeks, peak #1, and score 318. "Grease" (Frankie Valli) is right there at rank 20 with 22 weeks, peak #1, and score 279.

That pairing tells a familiar story from the theater line: screen moments did not stay on screen. They traveled into radio rotation and then right back into conversation. When music from film keeps that kind of chart visibility, it usually means the audience experience was collective and repeatable.

I like to think of 1978 as a year when people did not just watch a movie and move on. They carried pieces of it home, replayed them, quoted them, and sang them. You can hear that behavior in the chart traces themselves.
