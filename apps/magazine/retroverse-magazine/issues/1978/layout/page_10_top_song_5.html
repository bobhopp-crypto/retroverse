05. Kiss You All Over — Exile

Top Song #5

By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor

Some songs announce themselves in the title, and this one does exactly that. "Kiss You All Over" pairs direct lyric language with a polished, radio-ready structure that kept it present all year.

Its chart footprint is strong: score 417, peak #1, and 23 total weeks. That run tells us listeners did not treat it like a one-week novelty. They kept bringing it back.

It is a perfect example of 1978’s pop confidence: immediate chorus, emotional directness, and enough staying power to become part of the year’s soundprint.
