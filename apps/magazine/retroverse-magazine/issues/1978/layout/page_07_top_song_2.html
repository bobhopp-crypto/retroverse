02. Night Fever — Bee Gees

Top Song #2

By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor

The first seconds of "Night Fever" don't ask for attention — they set the temperature. The groove comes in smooth and certain, all polished surfaces and locked-in motion, like a club door swinging open just after eleven. By the time the vocal rises, the room already belongs to it.

“It doesn’t start the night — it owns midnight.”

This is the sound spilling out of discotheques after the line has finally moved inside. It's on FM radio during the drive downtown, in bar speakers before the floor fills, in theaters still running Saturday Night Fever, and in the cabs waiting outside long after midnight. Few records this year move so easily from screen to street.

What makes "Night Fever" stand out is its control. Where other hits charge at the chorus, this one glides there with absolute confidence. The falsetto stays cool above the beat, the rhythm section keeps the floor tight, and every phrase lands like it already knows exactly how the crowd will answer.

You feel it physically in the shoulders first, then in the legs. It has that precise disco pull: not frantic, not loose, just steady enough to keep a whole room moving in one direction. The song never hurries, which is exactly why nobody steps away from it.

That matters in a year crowded with hooks, novelty, and noise. "Night Fever" doesn't need a trick. It wins on atmosphere, polish, and nerve, turning nightlife into something sleek and inevitable.

If "Stayin' Alive" kicks the doors open, "Night Fever" keeps the lights low and the floor full. At No. 2, it doesn't sound like second place at all — it sounds like the hour when the party is fully underway.
