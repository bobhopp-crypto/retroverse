01. Stayin' Alive — Bee Gees

Top Song #1

By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor

The opening pulse on "Stayin' Alive" still feels like a signal flare for the whole year. In this context file, it lands at the top with a score of 503, a #1 peak, and 23 total weeks on chart. That is not just a hit. That is a season-long presence.

What makes it memorable is how confidently it arrives. The groove is immediate, the vocal line is unmistakable, and the title itself becomes a cultural phrase. You hear it once and you already know where the record wants to take the room.

This is the kind of song that explains 1978 in one spin: rhythm-first, high-visibility, and built for repeat listens.
