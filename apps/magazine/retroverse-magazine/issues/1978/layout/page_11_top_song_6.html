06. Can't Smile Without You — Barry Manilow

Top Song #6

By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor

By the time this chorus lands, the whole record feels like a conversation. "Can't Smile Without You" did not peak at #1, but its performance still stands out: score 382, peak #3, and 19 chart weeks.

This is where 1978 gets interesting. Not every defining song is a chart-topper. Some become fixtures because people keep returning to the sentiment.

The song’s tone is open and human-sized, and that emotional accessibility is exactly what gave it long life in the year.
