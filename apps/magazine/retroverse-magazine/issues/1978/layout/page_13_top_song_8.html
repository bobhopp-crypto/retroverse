08. Lay Down Sally — Eric Clapton

Top Song #8

By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor

"Lay Down Sally" moves with an easy confidence that still holds attention over time. Its context numbers are impressive: score 366, peak #3, and 23 chart weeks.

I like this one because it sounds relaxed without being passive. The groove is steady, the vocal delivery is unforced, and the record keeps its identity from first verse to last.

In a year full of high-energy highlights, this track proves that calm momentum can be just as magnetic.
