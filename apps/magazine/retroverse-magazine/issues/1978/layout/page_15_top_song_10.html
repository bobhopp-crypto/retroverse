10. Boogie Oogie Oogie — A Taste Of Honey

Top Song #10

By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor

"Boogie Oogie Oogie" sounds like motion before it sounds like explanation. That is part of its charm, and part of its chart strength: score 352, peak #1, and 23 weeks on chart.

The rhythm section does most of the talking here, and the title promises exactly what the record delivers. It is direct, playful, and built to pull a room together fast.

As a top-10 anchor, this song captures the physical energy that runs through so much of 1978.
