03. (Love Is) Thicker Than Water — Andy Gibb

Top Song #3

By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor

This record opens softer than some of the year’s loudest floor-fillers, but it does not drift. It holds. The context numbers say it plainly: score 466, peak #1, 20 chart weeks.

The song’s strength is balance. It feels romantic without becoming fragile, and melodic without becoming slight. By the chorus, it has the kind of familiarity that makes listeners lean in instead of tune out.

1978 had space for glitter and urgency, but it also had room for songs like this one: steady, heartfelt, and durable.
