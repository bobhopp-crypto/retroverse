# Culture Feature: Everyday 1978
*By Eve Binet, Culture & Lifestyle Editor*

Some years feel like a single headline. 1978 feels like a stack of little moments: a chorus stuck in your head on the walk home, a crowded store speaker, a living-room turntable, a weekend outfit planned around where the music might lead.

The top songs context tells us what people kept close. The top five by score are "Stayin' Alive," "Night Fever," "(Love Is) Thicker Than Water," "Three Times A Lady," and "Kiss You All Over." Even the titles map a social mood: motion, romance, confidence, and direct feeling.

And then there is the daily-life repetition. Long chart runs like 30 weeks for "Hot Child In The City" or 25 weeks for "Shadow Dancing" mean these tracks were not background noise for one season. They were woven into habits.

That is the culture angle I trust most: what people replayed, what they recognized instantly, and what kept showing up in ordinary life.
