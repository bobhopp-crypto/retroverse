Illustration role: SPOT_ARTIST
Studio responsibility: Handle small decorative, utility, and department pages with compact scene motifs that support layout without overwhelming copy.

Role-specific prompt framing:
- Treat the artwork as a compact editorial spot illustration or vignette that supports copy.
- Favor a simplified vignette, prop cluster, or single expressive figure over a dense full-scene tableau.
- Keep the composition modular and easy for the layout engine to overlay with text.
- Use concise storytelling detail rather than wide environmental sprawl.

Page purpose:
Present Masthead as a complete RetroVerse 1978 department masthead page, with page-aware artwork under real typography and no text baked into the illustration layer for `masthead`.

Style instructions:
- Favor simple props, vignettes, and atmospheric details over large cinematic action.
- Keep ornamentation modular so the layout engine can place typography cleanly on top.
- Treat the page as editorial support art, not a simulated finished advertisement or magazine spread.

Composition:
- full-page editorial support illustration with disciplined copy zones
- Orientation: portrait
- Mood: editorial and period-aware
- Color palette: warm paper stock, halftone black, amber, teal, and late-70s saturated accent colors

Visual hierarchy:
- Primary focal area supports Masthead.
- Secondary props reinforce `masthead` without competing with text zones.
- Negative space stays calm inside title, body, sidebar, and footer zones.
- Decorative elements remain subordinate to the primary text frame.

Illustration rules:
- no readable text inside artwork
- DO NOT RENDER FINAL TEXT INTO THE ARTWORK.
- Do not render readable article body text.
- Do not render chart or table text.
- Do not render fake production typography.
- Do not render magazine headlines, page titles, pull quotes, sidebars, or page furniture.
- Only environmental/signage text is allowed, and only if it is natural, minimal, and subordinate.
- Preserve open space for title, body, sidebar, and footer placement.
- Treat the artwork as a background asset used by the layout engine.

RetroVerse Easter egg rule:
- Optional and subtle only: a tiny RetroVerse Easter egg may appear as environmental detail, but it must never behave like final page typography.

Global artwork constraint:
- editorial illustration only, background artwork, no text, no typography, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no signage, no readable symbols. Leave clean open space near the top of the composition for magazine masthead placement.
