# Letters

### "You Could Hear It Everywhere"
**R. M., Cleveland**

I swear every place I went this year had "Stayin' Alive" playing at least once. I didn't mind. It felt like the city had one shared soundtrack.

---

### "Two Songs, Same Family"
**D. P., Sacramento**

Between Bee Gees and Andy Gibb showing up all over the list, this year felt like one long musical conversation. I love hearing how those songs sat next to each other.

---

### "Give Me the Slow Burn"
**L. H., Tulsa**

The flashy hits were great, but I kept returning to songs that stayed for months. Those long runs tell the real story of what people lived with.

---

### "Movie Songs on the Radio"
**J. F., Newark**

When "You're The One That I Want" and "Grease" kept climbing, it made movie nights feel like part of radio nights. That's the crossover I remember.

---

### "Thanks for the Top 23"
**S. K., Phoenix**

Love the closing countdown page idea. It feels like the final spin before the station signs off for the night.
