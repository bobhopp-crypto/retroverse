# 02. Night Fever — Bee Gees
*By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor*

"Night Fever" carries a title that feels like a whole weekend compressed into two words. The chart data backs up that feeling: score 490, peak #1, and 20 weeks on chart.

What I love about this one is its momentum. It sounds like movement from the first bar, then keeps its shape all the way through. The hook is clear, the vocal phrasing is crisp, and the record never loses its pulse.

If "Stayin' Alive" is the front door to 1978, "Night Fever" is the crowd still dancing after midnight.
