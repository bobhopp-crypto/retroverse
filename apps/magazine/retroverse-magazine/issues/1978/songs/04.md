# 04. Three Times A Lady — Commodores
*By Bob Jones, Music Features Editor*

"Three Times A Lady" arrives with confidence and patience. It does not rush its feeling, and maybe that is exactly why it connected. In the 1978 context set, it posts score 420 with a #1 peak across 20 chart weeks.

The record is warm, direct, and built around clarity rather than spectacle. Every line sounds like it is aimed at one person, even while the whole room hears it at once.

That mix of intimacy and scale makes this one of the year’s defining slow-burn centerpieces.
