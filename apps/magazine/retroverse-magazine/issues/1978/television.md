# Television of the Year
*By Linda Park, Television Editor*

Television in 1978 was still a weekly appointment, and the music context reflects that rhythm. Songs with long runs stayed in the public ear the way beloved programs stayed in the weekly schedule. In this dataset, "Hot Child In The City" reaches 30 weeks, while "Shadow Dancing" and "You Needed Me" each reach 25.

That kind of repeat contact matters. Shared viewing and shared listening both create the same social pattern: everyone has something in common to talk about the next day. It is less about one giant moment and more about reliable return.

So even with a chart-focused context file, you can still see the television-era habit underneath it: a nation moving in sync, one week at a time.
