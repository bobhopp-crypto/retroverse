Interview Feature: After Hours DJ

A booth-level conversation about tempo, crowd flow, and radio spillover.

Interview by A.J. Hunter

Feature Hero Image ../art/feature_dj_booth_placeholder.jpg

Interview format placeholder: the DJ reads rooms by minute, not by genre labels.

Q: What changes first when a floor cools? A: Not volume, sequence. You steer mood by transitions.

Q: How much does radio affect club requests? A: More every month. Hits arrive with expectations attached.

Q: What stays constant? A: People want momentum, surprise, and one song that resets the room.

"A great set feels inevitable only after it works."
