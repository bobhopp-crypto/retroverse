Department: Street Level Culture Notes

Prof Wheeler tracks behavior shifts in arcades, malls, and kitchens.

By "Prof" Dick Wheeler

Arcades now function as neighborhood observatories: one player at the controls, five analysts behind him.

Fashion divides by hour: daytime denim practicality, nighttime metallic confidence and platform ambition.

Home technology is moving from novelty to expectation, one appliance counter at a time.
