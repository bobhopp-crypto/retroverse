Feature: Disco and the Pushback

Popularity, taste battles, and the argument over what counts as "real" music.

By A.J. Hunter

Feature Hero Image ../art/feature_backlash_placeholder.jpg

Any sound that dominates this quickly attracts a counter-chorus, and disco is hearing it from every corner.

Some critics call it overproduced. Fans answer that craft and polish are part of the point, not a flaw.

Record stores now stock dance singles beside rock staples, creating a weekly referendum in the bins.

Backlash aside, the dance floor remains crowded, and the charts keep confirming demand.

"People argue about disco all week, then dance to it on Saturday."

Sidebar Facts

  • Regional radio formats are blending
  • Dance records cross into daytime rotation
  • Club playlists now influence retail sales
