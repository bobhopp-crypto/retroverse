Comic

Narrative Comic: Friday Circuit

Six panels tracking one night from record store to last call.

Storyboards by Hanna Judge | Script by RetroVerse Desk

Panel 1

Store clerk cues a new single over tiny monitors.

Caption: 7:12 p.m., the first crowd arrives.

Panel 2

Friends debate whether to start at the diner or the club.

Dialogue: "One song and we decide."

Panel 3

Taxi window frames neon and theater marquees.

Caption: Midtown moves in synchronized blur.

Panel 4

Dance floor opens and the room aligns to the kick drum.

Caption: The chorus lands; the line dissolves.

Panel 5

Late booth conversation about records, rent, and tomorrow.

Dialogue: "Play that one again before closing."

Panel 6

Dawn bus stop with shoes in hand and radio still on.

Caption: End of set, start of Saturday morning.
