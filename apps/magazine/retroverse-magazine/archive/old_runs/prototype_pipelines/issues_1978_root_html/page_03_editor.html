Editor's Intro: 1978 at Full Volume

The year feels transitional, but never quiet.

By B.J. Lovestreet

Spot Illustration ../art/editor_intro_portrait_placeholder.jpg

This year carries a specific hum: dance-floor bass, marquee chatter, and television voices drifting into every evening routine.

The late seventies are not replacing one culture with another. They are layering old habits with brighter production, faster pacing, and wider audiences.

RetroVerse is built to capture that motion in the present tense, one page at a time.

Quick Sidebar

  • Music keeps setting the public mood
  • Cinema is scaling up its spectacle
  • Television remains the weekly anchor
"1978 feels like a hinge year: not an ending, but a loud turn."
