Feature: Big-Screen Season

Why theatergoing still feels communal and event-sized in 1978.

By Charles Carter

Feature Hero Image ../art/feature_cinema_marquee_placeholder.jpg

Cinema this year thrives on contrast: rowdy comedy, sincere heroics, and suspense crafted for packed rooms.

A marquee now functions as a social bulletin board. Audiences show up for films and for each other.

Theater acoustics, scale, and crowd reaction still provide a texture that home viewing cannot replicate.

In 1978, moviegoing remains one of the clearest shared weekly rituals.

"A full theater can turn a good scene into a collective memory."

Sidebar Facts

  • Comedy pacing is getting faster
  • Spectacle releases drive repeat viewings
  • Sound design now carries more narrative weight
