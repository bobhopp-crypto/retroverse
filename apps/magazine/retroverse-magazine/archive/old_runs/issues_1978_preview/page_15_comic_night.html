Comic

Narrative Comic: Friday Circuit

From record shop opening to final bus stop.

Storyboards by Hanna Judge | Script by RetroVerse desk

Panel 1

Friday night starts at the record counter as friends compare new 45s.

The plan forms before sunset.

Panel 2

The DJ drops Hot Stuff and the room locks into tempo.

One song can reset the whole mood.

Panel 3

By midnight, the line wraps around the block for Superman.

Cinema becomes a group event.

Panel 4

Saturday afternoon drifts toward the arcade and a pocket of quarters.

Competition is neighborhood theater.

Panel 5

At home, everyone argues playfully over whether to watch WKRP in Cincinnati.

TV still sets the weekly rhythm.

Panel 6

Sunday closes with notes, flyers, and tickets spread on a kitchen table.

A year gets archived one weekend at a time.
