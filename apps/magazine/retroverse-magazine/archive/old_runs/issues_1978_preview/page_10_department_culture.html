Culture Notebook

Prof Wheeler on arcades, wardrobes, and appliances.

By "Prof" Dick Wheeler

Spot Illustration../art/feature/department_portrait.png

Arcades now act like social observatories where strategy and performance share the same floor space.

Fashion keeps splitting by setting: practical denim by day, metallic confidence by night.

Home technology keeps moving inward, from microwaves to cassette systems and new video format talk.
