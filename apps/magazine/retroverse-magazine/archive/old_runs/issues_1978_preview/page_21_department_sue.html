Sue Says: Mailbag

Dating, dignity, and dance-floor etiquette.

By Sue Belmer

Letter placeholder: every date feels like a dance audition, and conversation keeps getting postponed.

Sue answer: chemistry is not choreography. Good company listens before it judges.

Practical rule: split the evening between movement and talk, then watch behavior, not promises.

Quick Sidebar

  • Ask for the evening you want
  • Use humor without shrinking your standards
  • Choose partners, not scorekeepers
"You are dating a person, not auditioning for points."
