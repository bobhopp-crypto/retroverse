Editor's Intro: 1978 in Transition

The late seventies are shifting faster than our labels.

By B.J. Lovestreet

Spot Illustration../art/feature/department_portrait.png

The mood of 1978 is unmistakable: bright records, crowded cinemas, and television that remains stitched into nightly routine.

We are watching established forms evolve in public. Disco widens, rock adapts, and pop grows more polished without becoming predictable.

This issue tracks those shifts with pages designed to feel like a printed magazine, not a website article stream.

Quick Sidebar

  • Music is setting pace for the whole week
  • Film is scaling up event energy
  • Television remains the intimate medium
"1978 feels less like an ending than a loud midpoint."
